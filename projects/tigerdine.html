TigerDine

The TigerDine app provides information about dining locations found across the Rochester Institute of Technology campus.

Features:

  • See hours, descriptions, and current occupancy for all dining locations on campus
  • See daily specials served at each location
  • Check out what visiting chefs are on campus each day and where you can find them
  • Get notified when and where your favorites visiting chefs will be on campus (iOS only)
  • View the menu at each location, with support for allergies and other dietary restrictions
  • See the nutrition facts for menu items
  • Add widgets to your home screen to see a location's hours at a glance (iOS only)

To try and provide the best possible experience on both iOS and Android, I've opted to maintain two entirely separate versions of the app, one for each platform. This allows me to create a native app using the preferred libraries for each platform, those being SwiftUI on iOS and Jetpack Compose on Android. I try my hardest to make TigerDine blend in with your device in both cases, and making two separate native apps also helps to keep the size down: TigerDine for iOS is 4.5MB, and TigerDine for Android is 1.7MB (as of 2026-09-27).
